A real-time graphics pipeline in C++ and OpenGL

A solo renderer with physically based rendering and hardware-accelerated occlusion culling that holds 60+ FPS across more than 200 textured models, each shaded per light.

01

The goal

Render a scene with hundreds of textured models and multiple lights at a steady 60 FPS, with materials that respond to light realistically, all built by hand on top of raw OpenGL.

02

Constraints

  • Solo, alongside a full course load.
  • No engine: C++, OpenGL and GLSL only.
  • Every light is shaded per model, so the cost grows quickly with scene size.
03

What I built

Physically based rendering. Materials follow physically based shading, so metal, plastic and rough surfaces react to light consistently instead of being tweaked by hand per model.

Hardware-accelerated occlusion culling. The GPU checks which models are hidden behind others, and those are skipped, so shading time is only spent on what the camera can actually see. This is what keeps per-light shading affordable with 200+ models in the scene.

04

Result

The pipeline sustains 60+ FPS with more than 200 textured models, each with per-light shading.
