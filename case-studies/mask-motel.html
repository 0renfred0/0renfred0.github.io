Mask Motel: a horror game in 48 hours

Built with two teammates at Global Game Jam 2026 Singapore. I programmed the camera systems and weapon interactions, laid out the levels, and made the scope call that let us ship on time: 2D characters instead of 3D.

Role
Gameplay programmer, level layout, visual assets
Team
3, with JustHallowed and kulu
Timeline
48 hours, Jan 2026
Tech
Unity, C#

Playable right here. Click the game to give it keyboard focus.

The problem

Make a complete, playable game from nothing in one weekend. Our idea: you're a mask artist making ends meet by crafting human disguises for monsters. You take orders through your motel room's peephole, craft the mask at your work table and pass it through the hatch in the door, while the customers get rowdier.

02

Constraints

  • 48 hours, start to finish.
  • 3 people covering code, art, levels and audio.
  • Had to ship web and Windows builds to itch.io by the deadline.
03

The key decision

We weighed 3D characters, but modelling, rigging and animating them would have taken most of the weekend. I chose 2D characters instead, which kept the eerie look while freeing up time for gameplay, and then produced the supporting visual assets for the release myself.

04

What I built

  • Camera systems for moving around the motel room and looking through the peephole.
  • Weapon interactions for dealing with customers who get out of hand.
  • Level layout of the motel room.
05

Result

Mask Motel shipped on time and is live on itch.io with web and Windows builds. Players rated it 4.8 out of 5 on itch.io (5 ratings, November 2026).
